1 · Sales happen all day SmartMart tills record a sale every few seconds.
2 · Batch: collect In batch processing the sales pile up in a transaction file until the scheduled run.
3 · Batch: run at night At midnight the whole batch updates the master file. All day, stock records were out of date.
4 · Real time: stream Each sale is published to a Kafka topic and processed within seconds. Stock is always current.
5 · Choosing Batch: high throughput, cheap, but delayed (payroll, daily reports). Stream: low latency, more complex (stock, fraud alerts).
