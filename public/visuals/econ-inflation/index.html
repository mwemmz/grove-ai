1 · Starting point AD meets short-run AS at price level P₁ and output Y₁.
2 · Demand-pull Too much spending chasing too few goods: AD shifts right, prices and output rise.
3 · Cost-push Back to the start. Costs rise instead (wages, fuel, imported inputs): AS shifts left.
4 · Stagflation Prices rise and output falls at the same time.
5 · The policy dilemma Boosting AD restores output, but pushes prices higher still.
