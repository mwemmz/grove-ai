1 · Demand for kwacha Foreigners need kwacha to pay for Zambian exports like copper, and to invest in Zambia.
2 · Supply of kwacha Zambians sell kwacha to buy foreign currency for imports and to invest abroad.
3 · The exchange rate The value of the kwacha settles where demand meets supply.
4 · Copper prices rise More export earnings raise demand for kwacha: the curve shifts right and the kwacha appreciates.
5 · Fuel imports rise Back to the start. More kwacha is sold to buy dollars: supply shifts right and the kwacha depreciates.
