1 · A deposit A farmer deposits K100,000 at ZANACO. With a 10% reserve ratio, it keeps K10,000.
2 · A loan becomes a deposit ZANACO lends K90,000. The borrower pays someone who banks at Stanbic.
3 · Round after round Stanbic keeps K9,000 and lends K81,000. Each round is 90% of the last.
4 · The total Deposits add up to K100,000 × 1 ÷ 0.10 = K1,000,000. Money multiplier = 10.
5 · The limits Cash held outside banks, extra reserves and few good borrowers make the real multiplier smaller.
