1 · Why “big” data Too much (volume), too fast (velocity) and too mixed (variety) for one ordinary database.
2 · Sources and ingestion Data arrives from apps, sensors, logs and social media. Kafka, Flume and Sqoop bring it in.
3 · Storage HDFS, a data lake or a NoSQL store keeps it across many machines.
4 · Processing MapReduce or Spark do the work in parallel. YARN decides which job gets which machines.
5 · Analysis and visualisation Hive, Spark SQL and dashboards turn the results into answers people can use.
