1 · The cube Sales stored by three dimensions at once: region × product × quarter.
2 · Slice Fix one dimension to one value (quarter = Q1). You get a flat table: one slice of the cube.
3 · Dice Pick a range on several dimensions (Lusaka and Copperbelt, phones and laptops, Q1–Q2): a smaller cube.
4 · Roll-up Climb a hierarchy: regions add up to Zambia, quarters add up to the year. Less detail.
5 · Drill-down The opposite: open Q1 into January, February and March. More detail.
6 · Pivot Rotate the view: products become rows and regions become columns. Same numbers, new angle.
