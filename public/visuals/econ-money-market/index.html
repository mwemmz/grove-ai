1 · Money demand People hold less money when interest rates are high: (M/P)ᵈ = 1,000 − 100r.
2 · Money supply The Bank of Zambia fixes M = 1,000. With P = 2, real balances M/P = 500.
3 · Equilibrium 500 = 1,000 − 100r, so the interest rate settles at r = 5%.
4 · BoZ raises M to 1,200 M/P rises to 600: 600 = 1,000 − 100r, so r falls to 4%. Borrowing gets cheaper.
5 · Income rises More transactions raise money demand. The curve shifts right and r rises again.
