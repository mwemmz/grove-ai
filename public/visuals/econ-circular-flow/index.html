1 · Two sectors Households own the land, labour and capital. Firms use them to produce.
2 · The real flow Factor services go to firms. Goods and services come back to households.
3 · The money flow Firms pay incomes. Households spend them. Output = income = expenditure.
4 · Leakages Saving, taxes and imports leave the flow, so spending on local firms falls.
5 · Injections Investment, government spending and exports come back in. Balance: S + T + M = I + G + X.
