1 · Why AD slopes down A lower price level raises real wealth, cuts interest rates and makes exports cheaper.
2 · Three ranges of AS Flat with spare capacity (Keynesian), rising (intermediate), vertical at full employment (classical).
3 · AD rises, Keynesian range Idle resources are put to work: output rises, prices stay put.
4 · AD rises, intermediate range Bottlenecks appear: output and prices both rise.
5 · AD rises, classical range The economy is at full employment: only prices rise. That is inflation.
