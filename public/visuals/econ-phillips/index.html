1 · The trade-off Phillips (1958): when unemployment is low, wage inflation is high, and the reverse.
2 · Moving along the curve Government boosts demand: unemployment falls from 6% to 4%, inflation rises from 2% to 5%.
3 · Expectations catch up Workers expect 5% inflation and ask for higher wages. The curve shifts up.
4 · Back to the natural rate Unemployment returns to 6% but inflation stays at 5%. In the long run the curve is vertical.
5 · Stagflation A supply shock shifts the curve up again: high inflation and high unemployment together.
